## 2. Specialist conferences worth retaining

These are outside or additional to the supplied core list, but are meaningful targets when the paper directly fits the area.

### High-value specialist targets
- COLT — learning theory and theoretical ML.
- AISTATS and UAI — statistical, probabilistic and uncertainty-aware ML.
- RSS and CoRL — robotics, robot learning and embodied AI.
- L4DC — learning for dynamics and control.
- ISMB/ECCB and RECOMB — computational biology and bioinformatics.
- MICCAI — medical imaging and biomedical AI.
- AAMAS — multi-agent systems and autonomous agents.
- ECML PKDD — European ML and data mining.
- ACM Multimedia — multimedia and multimodal research.
- RecSys — recommender systems.
- ICASSP — speech, audio and signal-processing ML.
- NAACL and EACL — additional NLP targets.
- WACV — applied computer vision.

### Conditional specialist conferences
- CDC and ACC: keep for control, dynamical systems and automation work.
- SC: keep for high-performance computing, parallel AI and supercomputing.
- QIP: keep only for quantum information or quantum algorithms.
- FAccT: keep for fairness, accountability, transparency and responsible AI.
- CSCW and UbiComp/IMWUT: keep for human-AI interaction, collaborative systems and ubiquitous/edge AI.
- IEEE VIS: keep for visualization and visual analytics.
- CIDR: keep for database architecture and data systems, but treat its symposium model separately from conventional archival conferences.

These are not general-purpose additions to an AI submission list. Their value comes from direct subfield fit.

## 3. Journal additions worth retaining

### ML theory, statistics and optimization
- JMLR, TMLR, Annals of Statistics, JRSS Series B, JASA, Biometrika and Bernoulli.
- Mathematical Programming, SIAM Journal on Optimization (SIOPT), SIAM Journal on Mathematics of Data Science (SIMODS), SIAM Journal on Numerical Analysis (SINUM), SIAM Journal on Control and Optimization (SICON), SIAM Journal on Applied Mathematics (SIAP), Foundations of Computational Mathematics (FoCM).

### Pure mathematics — conditional only
- Annals of Mathematics, Journal of the American Mathematical Society (JAMS), Inventiones Mathematicae, Publications Mathématiques de l'IHÉS, Acta Mathematica, Journal of the European Mathematical Society (JEMS), and Communications on Pure and Applied Mathematics (CPAM).

Keep these only if the work is genuinely a proof-driven mathematical result. They are not ordinary machine-learning journals or routine targets for empirical ML papers.

### Theory, algorithms, information theory and security
- Journal of the ACM (JACM), SIAM Journal on Computing (SICOMP), IEEE Transactions on Information Theory, Journal of Cryptology.

### Systems, data, software and signal processing
- IEEE Transactions on Software Engineering (TSE), IEEE Transactions on Knowledge and Data Engineering (TKDE), IEEE Transactions on Parallel and Distributed Systems (TPDS), IEEE Transactions on Signal Processing (TSP).

### Robotics and control
- The International Journal of Robotics Research (IJRR), IEEE Transactions on Robotics (T-RO), IEEE Robotics and Automation Letters (RA-L), IEEE Transactions on Automatic Control (TAC), and IEEE Transactions on Control Systems Technology (TCST).

### Computational biology and biomedical engineering
- PLOS Computational Biology, Briefings in Bioinformatics, Journal of Computational Biology, IEEE Transactions on Biomedical Engineering.

### Physics and chemistry — conditional only
- Physical Review Letters (PRL), Physical Review X (PRX), PRX Life, PRX Quantum, Journal of Chemical Theory and Computation (JCTC), Chemical Science, Journal of the American Chemical Society (JACS), PNAS Nexus.

Use these only where the work establishes a genuine advance in the corresponding scientific domain. An ML method applied to a physics or chemistry dataset is not automatically a physics or chemistry discovery.

## 4. Items not retained as core research targets

- IEEE Signal Processing Magazine is not treated as a core original-research journal because it is primarily a magazine/tutorial/review outlet. IEEE Transactions on Signal Processing is retained for original research.
- No extra venue should be added just to inflate the directory. The Venue Directory labels conditional targets explicitly.
- Workshop papers and workshop proceedings must not be represented as main-track conference acceptances.

## 5. How to interpret the two source lists and acceptance rates

CSRankings and csconferences.org are distinct resources.

- CSRankings is a metrics-based ranking of computer science departments using faculty publications at a curated set of top venues. Its FAQ explains that the venue selection was developed with faculty input and that only a limited set of top conferences per field is included: https://csrankings.org/faq.html
- csconferences.org reports conference publication and acceptance-rate statistics. Its five-year mean acceptance rates are descriptive and should not be read as a universal prestige ranking: https://csconferences.org/

The tracker uses the supplied csconferences.org set as a mandatory coverage list. It does not claim that all those conferences are on CSRankings or that the acceptance rates determine admissions value.

## 6. PhD admissions interpretation

A paper's value depends on the contribution, how well it fits the target faculty's research area, the applicant's actual role, the evidence in recommendation letters, and the overall research trajectory. It is not generally valid to say that a first-author paper at one specialist venue always outweighs a later-author paper at a broad flagship venue. Committees need credible evidence of what the applicant personally did and how strong the work is.

A top specialist venue can be a better signal than a broad AI venue for a paper in that specialist area. It is not automatically better for every AI applicant.

## 7. Travel grant tracking

The Deadline Tracker has two conference columns:
- Travel grant available? (Yes / No / TBA)
- Travel grant deadline

Use Yes or No only after checking an official conference/student-funding page. TBA means unknown or not yet announced, not No. Include the source URL in Notes or the CFP source column. The automation issues a one-time notification two calendar days before an officially confirmed grant application deadline.


## 8. Additional specialist conferences retained

- **ITCS — Innovations in Theoretical Computer Science:** keep for substantial theory, algorithms, complexity and conceptual TCS contributions. ITCS 2027's official CFP lists abstract and full-paper deadlines of September 2 and 4, 2026, and conference dates January 12–15, 2027. Both submission deadlines have passed as of November 7, 2026. Source: https://itcs-conf.org/
- **IPDPS — IEEE International Parallel and Distributed Processing Symposium:** keep for parallel and distributed computing, HPC, distributed training, accelerators and AI systems. IPDPS 2027's official CFP lists October 1, 2026 for abstracts and October 8, 2026 for full papers; both have passed. Source: https://www.ipdps.org/ipdps2027/2027-call-for-papers.html
- **FM — International Symposium on Formal Methods:** keep conditionally for program verification, formal methods, logic, semantics and assurance of dependable systems. The 2027 edition is listed for October 18–22, 2027 in Athens; submission milestones should remain TBA until confirmed in the official CFP. Official event page: https://conf.researchr.org/home/fm-2027

These are additional specialist targets and are not part of the 66-conference csconferences.org list supplied by the user.
